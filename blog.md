# Blog
Throughout this course, I will write posts about data science topics I am interested in.
## Blog 1
Learning Data science in a social and interdisciplinary context matters because it can help us understand patterns in the world, especially when those patterns involve people, communities, and social issues. Modeling social topics can help researchers and organizations identify trends, make better decisions, and better understand problems that may be difficult to see without data. At the same time, working with data about people comes with a lot of responsibility. We have to think about privacy, bias, fairness, and whether the conclusions we draw accurately represent the people behind the numbers.

Throughout this course, I hope to better understand how data science can be used responsibly in social and interdisciplinary settings. I also want to learn how the choices we make when collecting, analyzing, and presenting data can influence the conclusions people take away from our work. My goal is to become better at not only analyzing data, but also thinking critically about the real-world impact of that analysis.
